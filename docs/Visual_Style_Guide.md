## Typography System

### Font Hierarchy

**HEADLINE FONT**: Futura Bold
- **Usage**: Main headlines, breaking news banners
- **Sizes**: 48px (desktop), 32px (tablet), 24px (mobile)
- **Weight**: Bold (700)
- **Tracking**: -1% for tight appearance
- **Line Height**: 1.1

**SUBHEADLINE FONT**: Arial Bold
- **Usage**: Segment titles, correspondent names
- **Sizes**: 32px (desktop), 24px (tablet), 18px (mobile)
- **Weight**: Bold (700)
- **Tracking**: 0%
- **Line Height**: 1.2

**BODY FONT**: Arial Regular
- **Usage**: Story text, descriptions, captions
- **Sizes**: 18px (desktop), 16px (tablet), 14px (mobile)
- **Weight**: Regular (400)
- **Tracking**: 0%
- **Line Height**: 1.5

**CAPTION FONT**: Arial Italic
- **Usage**: Lower thirds, image captions, quotes
- **Sizes**: 14px (desktop), 12px (tablet), 10px (mobile)
- **Weight**: Regular (400), Italic style
- **Tracking**: +1% for readability
- **Line Height**: 1.3

### Typography Rules

**HIERARCHY PRINCIPLES:**
- Never use more than 3 font sizes in one layout
- Maintain clear visual hierarchy through size and weight
- Use color to establish hierarchy, not just size
- Ensure line length doesn't exceed 75 characters for readability

**ACCESSIBILITY STANDARDS:**
- Minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text
- Minimum contrast ratio of 3:1 for large text (18px+)
- Avoid using color as the only indicator of meaning
- Provide text alternatives for all graphical text

## Lighting Design

### Studio Lighting

**ANCHOR LIGHTING:**
- **Key Light**: Soft, 45-degree angle, daylight balanced (5600K)
- **Fill Light**: Softer, 30-degree angle, 1 stop below key
- **Back Light**: Rim light from behind, 2 stops above key
- **Background Light**: Colored to match segment tone
- **Effect**: Professional, flattering, consistent

**CORRESPONDENT LIGHTING:**
- **Portable Setup**: LED panels with battery power
- **Key Light**: Larger, softer source for field conditions
- **Fill Light**: Reflector or secondary LED panel
- **Back Light**: Battery-powered rim light
- **Effect**: News-quality, adaptable to conditions

### Dramatic Lighting

**INVESTIGATIVE SEGMENTS:**
- **Style**: High contrast, shadows emphasized
- **Key Light**: Harder, more directional
- **Fill Light**: Minimal or absent
- **Back Light**: Strong rim for separation
- **Effect**: Serious, mysterious, dramatic

**HUMAN INTEREST STORIES:**
- **Style**: Warmer, softer, more intimate
- **Key Light**: Very soft, large source
- **Fill Light**: Nearly equal to key
- **Back Light**: Gentle, warm-toned
- **Effect**: Emotional, accessible, caring

## Motion Graphics

### Animation Principles

**NETWORK MOTION IDENTITY:**
- **Speed**: Moderate, deliberate, not rushed
- **Easing**: Smooth, professional transitions
- **Style**: Clean, modern, tech-forward
- **Consistency**: All elements follow same motion principles
- **Purpose**: Enhance content, not distract

**SPECIFIC ANIMATIONS:**
- **Logo Reveal**: Cosmic spiral formation (2 seconds)
- **Lower Thirds**: Slide-in with subtle overshoot (0.5s)
- **Transitions**: Cosmic dust or light movement (1-3s)
- **Data**: Sequential element reveal (paced to narration)
- **Weather**: Smooth particle movement (continuous)

### Technical Specifications

**RESOLUTION:**
- **Primary**: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- **High-End**: 3840x2160 (4K) for premium content
- **Mobile**: 1080x1920 (vertical) for social media

**FRAME RATE:**
- **Standard**: 30fps for news content
- **Premium**: 60fps for high-end segments
- **Social**: 24fps for cinematic feel

**CODEC:**
- **Production**: ProRes 422 for editing
- **Delivery**: H.264 for web, ProRes for broadcast
- **Archive**: Uncompressed or lossless for long-term storage

## Quality Standards

### Technical Quality

**VIDEO STANDARDS:**
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1080p, preferred 4K
- **Audio**: Clear, broadcast-quality, no background noise
- **Lighting**: Professional, well-exposed, consistent
- **Stability**: Tripod or stabilized footage
- **Composition**: Rule-compliant, visually pleasing

**GRAPHICS STANDARDS:**
- **Resolution**: Vector-based, scalable
- **Typography**: Consistent, readable, on-brand
- **Color**: Accurate, consistent, accessible
- **Animation**: Smooth, purposeful, professional
- **File Organization**: Clear naming, version control

### Content Quality

**EDITORIAL STANDARDS:**
- **Accuracy**: Fact-checked, verified, sourced
- **Balance**: Multiple perspectives represented
- **Clarity**: Complex topics explained accessibly
- **Relevance**: Timely, meaningful to audience
- **Ethics**: Clear distinction between fact and opinion

**PRODUCTION STANDARDS:**
- **Pacing**: Appropriate to content type
- **Flow**: Logical progression, smooth transitions
- **Engagement**: Maintains audience interest
- **Completion**: Satisfying resolution or setup
- **Consistency**: Matches network quality standards
